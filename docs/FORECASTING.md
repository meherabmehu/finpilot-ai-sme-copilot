# Forecast reliability and transparent baselines

Forecasts require known opening cash, paid history, one currency and user-confirmed complete cash records. Ordinary paid cash activity in up to 90 calendar days supplies daily average inflows/outflows; transfers and loan movements are not repeating operating activity. Future pending bills/collections and recurring commitments are added explicitly. Linked invoice/recurring occurrences are now counted once. Unlinked duplicate obligations and historical averages that already contain similar recurring costs cannot be safely inferred away; the UI warns of remaining overlap.

The historical forecast (7/15/30/90 days) is different from Decision Lab's dated-only commitments. It is an unvalidated moving-average estimate, not trained ML or a confidence interval. Both scheduled inflows and outflows remain visible even on days when their net is zero. Pending collections remain uncertain. Coverage under 30 calendar days or 10 active days is explicitly low-confidence; longer coverage is not proof of reliability. The adverse sensitivity assumes no future receipts at all, unchanged historical outflows plus scheduled payments; it is not assigned a probability.

Backtesting evaluates four historical seven-day cutoffs when at least 30 observations, 30 calendar days and 10 active days are available. Both forecast and backtest operating-average components use a 90-day window. Financing/transfers are excluded from training and the evaluated operating-cash target. Fold outputs include cutoff, window length, predicted/actual net cash and absolute error.

MAE is mean absolute weekly net-cash error. WAPE = sum(abs(predicted weekly net − actual weekly net)) / sum(abs(actual weekly net)) × 100. Zero denominator returns unknown, not zero error; denominator below 10% of gross cash movement is flagged as potentially unstable. This diagnostic threshold is transparent, not a statistical confidence bound. A business can have high turnover but near-zero net cash, making net-WAPE large even when an absolute error seems modest.

The evaluator does NOT reconstruct as-known historical invoice schedules/status changes, evaluate the complete schedule-overlay forecast, or validate 15/30/90-day error. Current records may have been edited/backdated since the historical cutoff; ledger-date walk-forward checks are not immutable historical forecast snapshots. No predictive improvement is claimed. The manually reported 1276.88 MAE / 71.41% WAPE cannot be independently reproduced without the exact dataset and cutoff. Irregular trading, sparse history, baseline/schedule overlap and small net denominators are risks, not asserted causes for that particular result.

Known-answer regressions verify linked schedule deduplication, gross flow display, no-receipt sensitivity and a synthetic stable operating flow yielding zero weekly baseline error. A constructed perfect synthetic baseline is not evidence of general business forecasting accuracy.

Legacy scenario simulations remain explicit last-30-day operating changes; marketing never implies automatic revenue uplift. Saving simulations changes no accounting records.
