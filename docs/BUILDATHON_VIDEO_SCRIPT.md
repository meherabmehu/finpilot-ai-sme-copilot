# English demo script — approximately 2 minutes 40 seconds

> Production update: the completed 2:53 video and the exact narration actually used
> are documented in [DEMO_VIDEO_DELIVERY.md](DEMO_VIDEO_DELIVERY.md). The timings
> below are the earlier outline. No YouTube upload has been made.

**0:00–0:20 — Problem**

“Imagine you freelance while studying. You have money in your account, essential bills
coming soon, and a client payment that might arrive late. A monthly total cannot tell
you whether you can spend today. Expected income is not available cash.”

**0:20–0:40 — Product**

“This is FinPilot AI, a personal and micro-business financial survival copilot. I
extended my existing SME application with a dedicated Personal Mode. Your personal
records and business workspaces stay separate. This public demo uses fully synthetic
data and needs no account.”

**0:40–1:05 — Dashboard**

“Our synthetic freelancer has eighteen thousand taka available, twelve thousand in
upcoming bills, ten thousand expected from a client, and an eight-thousand-taka
emergency reserve target. Cash after bills, excluding future receipts, is only six
thousand. The conservative spending room is zero. The app does not spend an unpaid
promise on your behalf.”

**1:05–1:35 — Scenario**

“Now I delay the selected income by seven days. Ending cash is still sixteen thousand,
but the lowest balance drops to six thousand before the essential bill deadline.
That creates a two-thousand-taka reserve shortfall. The same monthly ending balance
can hide a very different week. I can also test an unexpected expense, a savings
transfer, or reduced future income. None of these simulations changes the ledger.”

**1:35–2:05 — Genuine AI**

“Here is an isolated synthetic private workspace showing the real Groq Advisor.
I ask what happens if my freelance payment arrives seven days late. The financial
engine calculates the figures; Groq explains relevant evidence and conditional
trade-offs. AI does not create amounts or execute payments. If the provider fails,
the app says so and uses deterministic analysis. The account-free public demo's
explanations are clearly labeled deterministic.”

**2:05–2:25 — Readiness**

“You can prioritize explicitly essential bills, correct your records and download a
genuine PDF, CSV or spreadsheet report. The consumer workflow is tested at mobile,
tablet and desktop widths, while the existing business functionality is preserved.”

**2:25–2:40 — Close**

“FinPilot helps people make a clearer decision before spending. It combines personal
financial context, transparent arithmetic, scenario planning and grounded AI. Try
the public synthetic demo at the link on screen.”

## Recording notes

Use only marked synthetic workspaces in screenshots/video. Never record `.env`,
passwords or real financial records. Use the live public demo for the account-free
sequence and clearly identify the isolated synthetic environment for private AI.
Do not imply a deterministic demo answer is a model response. The completed video
records the public synthetic workflow and labels the saved private Groq response.
