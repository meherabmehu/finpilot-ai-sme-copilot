## Product Name & Brief

FinPilot AI – Financial Survival Copilot

FinPilot AI helps freelancers and small business owners understand their available
cash, anticipate possible shortages and compare financial decisions before acting.
Personal Mode separates received cash, expected income, essential bills and an
emergency reserve. Users can simulate delayed income, unexpected expenses, savings
transfers and reduced future income. A private Groq-powered AI Advisor provides
explainable guidance grounded in authorized records, while deterministic calculations
provide the financial figures. Separate personal and business workspaces, responsive
dashboards and downloadable reports support practical planning. An account-free demo
lets judges explore the workflow using clearly labeled synthetic data.
