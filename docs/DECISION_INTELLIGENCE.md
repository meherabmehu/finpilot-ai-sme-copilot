# Decision intelligence

Open Financial Planning → Decision Lab, choose an issue card or enter a question. Owners may create a saved investigation; viewers may read saved evidence only. The result explains recorded cash, dated-only estimates, original timeline, comparison ranking, constraints, dependencies and limitations.

Each simulated plan shows minimum cash/date, reserve gap, payment-first risk, borrowing, full financing cost, beyond-horizon liabilities and action count. “Within action constraints” does not mean a plan closes its funding gap or counterparties have agreed. Recommendation means best among the bounded evaluated alternatives, not global optimality or financial advice.

Record a plan, reject it with feedback, or build a modification with up to three action rows. Advanced JSON accepts up to eight actions. Modified proposals are recalculated on the original snapshot; acceptance requires unchanged current evidence. Identical modification retries do not duplicate lifecycle events. Acceptance never edits transactions. Saved modified results and expected outcomes remain inspectable in the detail screen and exports.

Financing calculations use owner-entered assumptions and historical net cash for a limited affordability indicator. A +2 percentage-point sensitivity is illustrative, not a forecast of current lender rates. Expired/inactive catalog products are filtered. Product matching and metadata are not independent lender verification or approval.
