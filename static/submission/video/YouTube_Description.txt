Can an expected client payment still leave you short before a bill deadline?

FinPilot AI is a Financial Survival Copilot for freelancers and small business owners. It helps users understand available cash, protect essential payments and compare financial decisions before acting.

WHAT THIS DEMO SHOWS
• Personal Mode: paid cash, expected income, essential bills and emergency reserves.
• Scenario planning: compare delayed income, unexpected expenses, savings transfers and reduced future income.
• A timing insight: the same ending balance can hide an interim reserve shortfall.
• Explainable AI guidance grounded in authorized financial evidence.
• Separate personal/business workspaces, mobile layouts and report exports.

TECHNOLOGY

DEMO TRANSPARENCY
All financial examples and screenshots use synthetic records. The account-free public demo uses deterministic explanations; the private AI segment shows a saved, verified Groq response from a synthetic workspace. Financial figures come from calculated evidence. AI cannot execute payments or move money. English narration is AI-generated and is not the creator's voice.

FinPilot AI provides decision support based on recorded data and assumptions. Projections are conditional estimates, not guarantees.
